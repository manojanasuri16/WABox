WABox keeps the WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business statuses you've viewed in one calm, fast gallery, so you can save the ones that matter before they disappear.

**Features**

- **WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business** discovered automatically, kept as separate sources you can rename ("Personal", "Work") and switch between
- **One-tap saving** to your gallery (`Pictures/WABox` + `Movies/WABox`, or `DCIM/WABox`) with duplicate detection
- **Full-screen viewer**: pinch and double-tap zoom, swipe between statuses, swipe down to close, video with scrubber and mute, share, details
- **Saved gallery** with photo and video tabs, date sections, and multi-select share and delete
- **Live updates** while the app is open, and new statuses show up without a refresh
- **Three home-screen widgets**: Recent statuses (with quick save), Account switcher, and Quick access
- **App Lock**: fingerprint, face, or screen lock, with a re-lock timeout and hidden recents preview
- Light and dark themes, plus optional background refresh

**Private by design**

- No internet permission and no ads. Nothing leaves your phone.
- No root and no "All files access". You grant only the WhatsApp folder, through Android's own file picker.

**Open source:** the full source code is public at https://github.com/manojanasuri16/WABox

**Requirements:** Android 10 to 16.

**Good to know:** only statuses WhatsApp has already downloaded (usually the ones you've viewed) can be shown. Two accounts inside a single WhatsApp app can be shown separately only if WhatsApp stores them in different folders.

_WABox is an independent app and isn't affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta._
